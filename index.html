07 / About
Half analyst, half artist, and convinced the two make each other better.

Economics taught me to look for the pattern underneath the noise: why people choose what they choose, and what the numbers say about it. I enjoy the practical side of that most, building a tracker that saves a team hours, or a dashboard that makes a decision obvious.

Away from spreadsheets, I sketch, paint and photograph. A mandala and a well-structured dataset have more in common than you might think: both reward patience, precision and an eye for balance. I bring that same care to every project, analytical or creative.
